The PDI Parga System

The PDI Parga Diesel Injection precisely injects timed diesel additive fluid directly into your diesel tank filler neck to provide completely splash-free additive delivery. This advanced system delivers superior diesel injection pump protection with added lubrication for longer engine life and peak performance.
