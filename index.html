What vehicles is the PDI Parga compatible with?

The PDI Parga Diesel Injection System fits any modern diesel-powered vehicle with a standard filler neck (passenger cars, SUVs, light trucks, and most agricultural equipment).

How often does the additive reservoir need refilling?

Typically after every 10–15 tank fill-ups, depending on your tank size. The system includes a low-level indicator light.

What is the warranty on the PDI Parga system?

Every PDI Parga unit comes with a 2-year limited manufacturer's warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship.

Can I install it myself?

Yes. Installation takes around 20 minutes with basic tools and does not require professional mechanical knowledge. A full step-by-step guide is included in the box.
